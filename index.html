common questions

Got questions?

Is Nourish available now?
Yes! Nourish is live on the App Store for iPhone. Download it free today. Android is coming soon.
Is Nourish free?
Nourish has a generous free tier with access to the full recipe library and Chef Nouri (5 messages per week). Nourish Pro unlocks unlimited Chef Nouri, full health tracking, and all premium features for $7.99/month or $59.99/year — less than one skipped takeout meal.
What is Nourish Pro?
Nourish Pro unlocks unlimited Chef Nouri AI messages, unlimited recipe saves, unlimited fridge scans, and all achievement badges. Try it free for 7 days — $7.99/month or $59.99/year after that.
What diets and allergies does Nourish support?
Nourish supports gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan, vegetarian, keto, paleo, nut-free, egg-free, carnivore, and more. But what makes Nourish different is that it doesn't just filter recipes — it actually adapts them. Set your restrictions once and Nourish rewrites ingredient lists and cooking steps to fit your exact needs, for every recipe in the app.
What is Chef Nouri?
Chef Nouri is your built-in AI kitchen assistant. Ask it to plan your week, suggest meals based on what's in your fridge, modify any recipe for your diet, or give you instant dinner ideas when you're stuck at 5pm. Chef Nouri knows your restrictions, your cooking history, and your preferences — every suggestion is actually relevant to you.
How many recipes does Nourish have?
Nourish launches with hundreds of handpicked home cooking recipes. This is just the beginning — we add new recipes regularly and our goal is thousands over time. You can also import any recipe from the web or create your own.
Is Nourish available on Android?
Nourish is currently available on iPhone. Android/Google Play is coming soon. Stay tuned!
Where can I follow along?
We're building Nourish in public on TikTok and Instagram @thenourishapp. Follow along for behind-the-scenes updates, early feature previews, and cooking content.
